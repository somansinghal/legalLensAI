1. Purpose & Scope of the Application

LegalLens AI is an artificial intelligence-assisted legal document reading and preparation platform designed to help individuals, freelancers, and small business owners understand complex contract terms before signing. The application provides plain-language clause explanations, obligation summaries, deadline tracking, and tailored consultation questions.
