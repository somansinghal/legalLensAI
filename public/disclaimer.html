3. Limitations of Artificial Intelligence

LegalLens AI utilizes advanced Large Language Models to read and analyze text. While these models are highly capable, they possess inherent technical limitations:

  • AI models may misinterpret subtle legal wording, jurisdictional conditions, or cross-referenced appendices.
  • AI models do not possess real-time awareness of evolving judicial case law, state-level statutory amendments, or judicial precedents unless explicitly provided in the contract text.
  • Attention ratings (e.g. HIGH ATTENTION, REVIEW CAREFULLY) represent machine-generated heuristics to guide human review and should never be taken as a conclusive legal determination of clause validity or enforceability.

4. Intended Workflow: "Understand Before You Sign"

LegalLens AI is designed to support, not replace, human legal expertise. The intended usage pattern is:

  1. Understand: Read the plain-language executive summary and clause breakdowns to gain situational clarity.
  2. Identify: Note your obligations, critical deadlines, and potential ambiguity.
  3. Prepare: Review the generated "Questions for a Legal Professional" section.
  4. Consult: Take those focused, informed questions to a licensed attorney before executing any binding document.
