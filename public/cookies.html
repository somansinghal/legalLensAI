Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026. LegalLens AI respects your digital privacy and utilizes only strictly necessary authentication cookies and client theme settings.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your computer or mobile device by websites that you visit. They are widely used to make web applications function efficiently, preserve authenticated states, and provide basic interface preferences.

2. Cookies Used by LegalLens AI

LegalLens AI maintains a strict, transparent cookie inventory. We use only one functional cookie:

Cookie Identifier Category Lifespan Purpose
legallens_session Strictly Necessary 4 hours Authenticates your session to access the protected workspace. Configured with HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and Secure in production to protect against cross-site scripting (XSS).

3. Browser Local Storage

In addition to our session cookie, LegalLens AI utilizes browser localStorage exclusively for user experience preferences:

  • legallens_theme: Stores your chosen visual theme (dark, light, or system). This prevents jarring flashes of dark or light backgrounds when navigating between pages. No sensitive tokens, credentials, or document excerpts are ever stored in localStorage.

4. What We DO NOT Use

In adherence to our strict privacy stance:

  • We do NOT use third-party advertising cookies or ad networks.
  • We do NOT use behavioral tracking pixels or cross-site tracking beacons.
  • We do NOT monetize, sell, or share user analytics with commercial data brokers.

5. Managing Cookies in Your Browser

You can manage or disable cookies through your web browser's security settings. However, please note that disabling the legallens_session cookie will prevent you from signing in or utilizing the document analysis workspace.
