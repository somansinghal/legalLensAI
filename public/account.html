🛡 Strict Privacy Guarantee

LegalLens AI is architected with a strict privacy boundary:

  • Zero Raw Text Persistence: Your uploaded or pasted contract text is processed ephemerally in server memory and is never written to Cloud Firestore or persistent storage.
  • Isolated Structured Output: Only validated AI metadata (clause explanations, attention ratings, and checklists) are stored in your private Firestore subcollection.
  • No Model Training: Processed documents are never used for machine learning model training or public evaluation.
